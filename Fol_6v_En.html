Foliu[m]/Blat VIv

A contemporary English translation is presented from comparing the existing Latin, Early New High German (ENHG) and English (translation) texts for better comprehensibility. Especially since the ENHG text is difficult to understand compared to modern High German, as a profound knowledge of German (as a mother tongue) as well as already aknowledge of various dialects is required.

The first age of the world

According to the Hebrews, the first age of the world, from Adam to the Flood, comprised 1656 years, while according to the Septuagint, Isidore and many others, it was 2242 years, which consequently became the standard time reckoning.

The supreme goodness1 wishes to impart this goodness to others, therefore it created a rational being who would understand the supreme goodness and thereby love it2, possess it [the supreme goodness] through this love and be blessed3.4

God now5 created the first man by forming his body with the help of the angels from the clay of the earth in the field of Damascus and inspiring7 his face with the breath of life6, that is8, he created a soul9 which he united with the formed body. Thus, man was created in his natural attributes in the image10 of God and in his spiritual gifts11 in his likeness12. Behold, the Lord bestows wonderful favor13 upon us.

Cu[m] ergo mare[m] ad si[mi]litudine[m] sua[m] primum finxisset: tu[m] etia[m] feminam [con]figurauit adipsius hominis effigiem vt dou inter se p[er]mixti sex[us] p[ro]pagare sobolem possent: [et] omne[m] terra[m] multitudine opplere.14

FOrmatis a[n]i[m]antib[us] terre [et] volatilib[us] adduct[is] ad Ada[m] vt videret ea: cu[m] ada[m] no[n] i[n]ueniret[ur] adiutor si[mi]lis sibi: immisit d[omi]n[u]s sopore[m] in Ada[m] [et] tulis una[m] de costis ei[us]: reple[n]s carne[m] pro ea: [et] edificauit in muliere[m]. Qua[m] ada[m] vide[n]s: dixit h[oc] nunc os de ossib[us] meis h[oc] vocabit[ur] Issa q[uo]d lati[n]e m[u]l[ie]r interp[re]tat[ur]: q[uorum] de viro su[m]pta e[st].15

After God created Adam, he led him to Paradise and there made Eve from the sleeping man's rib16, thus forming him a companion. He made her neither from the head17, so that she would not rule over the man, nor from the foot of the man, so that she would not be spurned, but from the side18, so that the bond of love would prove itself19.20

This also shows that it is not the value21 of the place of origin, but one's own goodness22 that gives each individual23 person moral character24. The man, created outside of Paradise, proves, although created in a lesser place25, to be better for26 Eve, who was created in Paradise.27

God, the supreme creator of all things, thus created on the sixth day of the world, on March 25th28, after the animals of the earth, all the reptiles and birds, Adam, the first created29, first man, from a red clod of earth or clay30 in the field of Damascus31, as the completion32 and possessor33 of all creatures.
